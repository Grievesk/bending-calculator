Technical notebook

Technical notes on sheet metal bending

From theoretical calculation to the real part

Grievesk Bend aims to improve bending calculations by starting from the geometry actually produced in the workshop.

A consistent K-factor matters, but it is not the whole story. Before deriving K and calculating the flat pattern, the model needs to estimate the inside radius the sheet will have after bending.

The formed radius can differ from the CAD radius and the nominal punch radius. That difference can affect development, fit between components and final dimensional accuracy.

These notes collect workshop observations and comparisons with CAD/CAM data. We distinguish direct measurements, CAM-derived values and model estimates; missing or unverified data are identified rather than invented.

The theory provides a model. Real data help establish how well it describes the manufactured part.
